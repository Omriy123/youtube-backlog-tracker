# Organize your Library

Save links with the toolbar's **Save to Inbox** action, then open the Library. Inbox holds videos awaiting a placement decision. Create watch lists for videos you intend to watch soon, or move reviewed videos to Backlog. **Entire Library** shows active videos across those destinations; viewing it does not move or duplicate anything.

## Lists and moves

Create and rename watch lists from the Library controls. Names must be nonempty and unique, ignoring case and surrounding spaces. Inbox and Backlog are permanent. Importance ranks your watch lists; browsing them by another overview sort preserves that ranking.

Use **Move video** or the selection panel's Move action to inspect the actual **Video / From / To** table. Choose one shared destination or different destinations for individual rows. **Keep in the current list** leaves that row's placement and dates unchanged. Ordinary moves do not offer Inbox.

Arriving videos fit around a destination's saved arrangement using publication dates, without rearranging its existing videos. Unknown dates go last. A pending rediscovery needs an explicit review decision before movement; completing that review leaves the move waiting for a separate confirmation.

## Sort and arrange

Click the sorting headers to browse by publication, title, channel, duration, destination or Library dates. Click the current header again to reverse its direction. Missing values stay last. Publication direction is remembered for each list.

**Manual** returns to the saved arrangement. Drag a row or use its Place control to choose a position. Arranging an unfiltered list adopts its displayed order and saves the result as Manual. Arranging filtered results is temporary: clearing or changing the filter restores the underlying saved order. Sorting alone preserves Manual.

## Search and channels

Combine search, a channel and the current list, or expand the same search to Entire Library and return to its originating list. Explicit list navigation keeps your query and channel. Clear the filters if they leave no matches.

Personal, Original, Intermediate and Latest search scopes control retained title and description versions. Historical matches can be inspected with their recorded source and observation time. Turning off every scope leaves URL-only search. Channel identity comes from recorded metadata; missing identity appears as **Channel unknown**.

Channel shortcuts keep their positions through search and scope changes. Their counts show what each channel would match with the other filters applied. Expand the shortcuts to find more channels. Destination headings in grouped Library results open that list while retaining filters.

## Collect a selection

Select videos across searches, channels and lists. **Select all** adds all current matches, including offscreen results, while preserving earlier selections elsewhere. Unchecking it removes only current matches. The main-area selection panel shows readable titles and From destinations, grouped as **In here** and **Elsewhere**.

Choose **All selected** or **Matches current filters** to set the panel action's affected set. **Show only selected** combines with your existing filters; turning it off preserves them. Clear the selection or deselect individual videos whenever needed.

An ordinary drag carries one video. Ctrl+drag on a selected result carries selected current results in their displayed order; selections elsewhere stay untouched. Arranging keeps the selection. Successful moves and removals deselect processed videos; failures and deliberately excluded pending rows remain selected for retry.

## Delete a list or remove matches

Whole-list deletion previews every active member, including videos hidden by filters. Explicitly choose to dismiss all or move all to a chosen destination. This full-list move may deliberately choose Inbox. An empty custom list still needs confirmation.

Matching-only cleanup affects the current results and keeps the list and its other videos. It follows ordinary Move destination restrictions. Dismissed records retain their earlier activity; deletion is not permanent record erasure.

Deleting your current or originating list opens the chosen move destination, or Entire Library after dismissal or empty-list deletion. Your filters remain available, including when they produce zero results.

## Recovery and preferences

Use the organization recovery control to reverse a recorded list edit, importance change, move, saved arrangement or deletion. A batch recovers as one action. Recovery preserves unrelated later decisions and automatic observations; a newer conflicting decision blocks reversal with an explanation.

Organization and its history save together. A failed save leaves the previous durable state intact and keeps the action available for retry. If an affected video or destination changes in another window, inspect the refreshed preview and confirm again.

Density, Rows/Columns, sort choices, overview sort and publication directions survive reopening. Search, selection, selected-only filtering and temporary arrangements clear when the Library reopens. Controls remain available through resizing and keyboard navigation.

Opening and rediscovery retain their [existing behavior and repair controls](first-slice-reference.md). Opening marks a video watched; it does not verify playback. A known opened tab whose Library update failed remains recoverable without opening another tab.
